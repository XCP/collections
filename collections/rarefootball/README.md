## Boundaries and attribution

Both player and manager cards are included, along with separately issued visual variants such as the Pele, Maradona and Vinicius cards. Football-themed works with different designs are excluded: BARCA, BENFICA, FCBAYERN, FCPEPE, INTERMIAMI, STEAUA, SUPERLEAGUE and FAKEAWARDS.

Despite its name, the asset RAREFOOTBALL is a different artwork: its metadata calls it 6529 Football Of Production, and it does not use the portrait-card design. It is excluded. Unrelated work from this issuer also remains outside the collection.

No artist is assigned. The metadata owner fields contain team names, countries and awards (for example ITALIA, Real Madrid and Not Ballon D'or), so they are not reliable creator credits. No verified project homepage or creator social profile was found. Similar names on unrelated merchandise sites are not evidence.

These 35 works move out of the Counterparty catch-all. No membership in another official directory is implied.
