## Membership and attribution evidence

The [project-operated website](https://testgalleryart.wordpress.com/) names artist jl, explains its Runway AttnGAN process, lists the twenty numbered genesis artworks, and identifies SMOKINGWEED as the completion reward and TESTGALLERY as the collection token. All 22 CDN previews were inspected. Each artwork has matching live [Coindaddy metadata](https://xcp.coindaddy.io/SMOKINGWEED.json) explicitly naming TESTGALLERY and signing jl. The collection-token image is the twenty-artwork contact sheet; it is included without an inferred artist credit.

All 22 assets were first issued on 2021-11-06 according to xcpio-core issuance history. The TEST asset itself dates earlier and is a dedication subject, not an artwork member. RPLACE, SOLARYEAR, TESTRESULTS and OEPEPE share this issuer but are not admitted.

The website numbers WRITINGMUSIC 12, correcting copied WINDOWVIEW text in that JSON description. Its WOMENINBED heading is a spelling error: the registered identifier, metadata, and collection-token image use WOMANINBED. Registry identifiers remain exact. The 20-member contact-sheet artwork provides independent visual confirmation of the numbered set.
