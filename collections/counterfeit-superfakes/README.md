## Membership evidence

The actual subasset longname in xcpio-core is KEKBONDS.KEKBONDS.PEPEGANDACARD; this is the single matching issued child under the reviewed issuer.

The article explicitly credits XCHAINPEPE to Rare Scrilla. That credit is added to its Rare Pepe primary membership and this secondary membership. The other three retain NORMANCOMICS credits and leave Counterparty.
