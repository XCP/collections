## Reviewed membership

The ten reviewed assets MOPPYA through MOPPYJ share an issuer, were created on January 26, 2017, and share the same stored squirrel mascot image. Ceres announced the Counterparty campaign on February 10, 2017. The matching mascot, timing, namespace and ten-card campaign support this attribution; the announcement does not enumerate the exact tickers.

Sources:
- [Official Ceres campaign announcement](https://ceres-inc.jp/news/detail/20170210/) (also the collection website).
- [Official campaign illustration](https://ceres-inc.jp/assets/images/news/20170210-img/image.png).
- [Contemporary campaign guide](https://motokase.com/moppy-go-matome/) and its [ten-card campaign graphic](https://motokase.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/moppy-card-10.jpg).

Ceres and IndieSquare are project operators. No individual illustrator has been established, so no Artist trait is inferred. Original IndieSquare metadata URLs no longer resolve.

This is a reviewed static membership snapshot. Additional prefix matches are not automatically admitted. All 10 entries have stored CDN artwork. These assets use this collection as their primary home and are removed from the Counterparty catch-all.
