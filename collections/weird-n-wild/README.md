The [creator's catalogue and history](https://jpjanssen.com/weird-n-wild/) explicitly names these six cards. This is their specific primary collection; existing Kaleidoscope appearances remain secondary under its documented overlap policy. No Counterparty fallback memberships are added.

JP Janssen is credited for assembling and tokenizing these cards, not for drawing the underlying illustrations or taking the CHURCHHILL photograph. His account identifies the five animal/creature designs as sourced from [Weird n’ Wild Creatures](https://weirdnwildcreatures.fandom.com/wiki/Weird_n%27_Wild_Creatures_Wiki); Source artwork traits preserve that distinction. His description of the source licensing is not an independent rights determination. No original illustrator names are inferred.
