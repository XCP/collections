## Membership evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-09 against xcpio-core issuer records and CDN contact sheets. These 88 early-2023 cards share the same issuer, dense textured collage treatment, repeated meme backgrounds, trading-card text panels and typography. Many use SHARP in their asset name, but the reviewed list also includes matching cards without that word.

The project name is grounded in the [SHARPS token artwork](https://cdn.xcp.io/img/card/SHARPS), which explicitly describes buying sharp cards. The same issuer's SHARPCLOSED asset links on chain to https://sharps.wtf/CARDS_files/TOKENclosed_0.png. The domain was unavailable during review, so no live website or complete historical directory is claimed. This explicit membership list is an editorial reconstruction based on the issuer, dated series and inspected artwork, not a recovered official checklist.

## Boundaries

All 95 assets from issuer 411648 were checked against the visual series. INVESTINPEPE, KEXC and SHRIMPYIELD are earlier, differently designed works and retain their Dank Rares memberships. NOCOINPEPE and SWAMPFROGS remain outside this list pending stronger series evidence. SHARPCLOSED shows a CDN placeholder and is excluded. SHARPS describes a currency used to buy the cards and is not included in this artwork list.

AbstractUnknown's separate earlier issuer 355624 is not included. All 88 selected works retain their existing AbstractUnknown credit and leave the Counterparty catch-all. Their current descriptions point directly to media; they are not claimed as artist JSON evidence.
