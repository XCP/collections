## Artwork chronology, reviewed 2026-10-13

KOKO was registered on 2015-09-25 with the description KOKO. On 2021-11-16 ownership transferred, the gorilla image was added, NATURE, KITTEN and CAT were issued with their matching named images, and supply was locked. SOS and FAKE followed on 2021-11-23; JEALOUS on 2021-11-30. ATOMO first appeared with its image on 2022-01-05 and retains 2022 as its individual art year.

Use 2021 for the documented emergence of this artwork family, supported by the coordinated ownership, media, supply and subasset events. This does not claim an exact date when the artist drew the work. Asset registration dates remain chain facts, separate from project and artwork dates. Existing creator credits are unchanged.
