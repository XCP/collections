## Original-token evidence

The artist’s [February 2015 catalog](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=954740) lists these two exact original token IDs with their image hashes and identifies issuing address 15E5UWodQ33MYfUDkShaWA68hFrg8MJg8v. Current chain-linked [A16727 JSON](http://davidsterry.com/daba/A16727.json) and [A34716 JSON](http://davidsterry.com/daba/A34716.json) independently repeat the exact identifiers, hashes, and reference photos. Both original tokens are already tracked with CDN media. Credit the artist’s published name Weex.

This is currently a partial registry of the fifteen originals. The other thirteen original token identifiers and 2015 issuance facts are corroborated by the catalog and KETF announcement, but their CDN previews are unavailable. Their reference-image recovery remains pending; do not substitute later WEEX subassets for them. The originals’ current small CDN icon is shared; the chain-linked JSON points to distinct full reference photos.

The separate WEEX Backups collection explicitly represents later reproductions and does not convey provenance as an original token or a current physical-redemption promise.
