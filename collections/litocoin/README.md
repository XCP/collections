## Reviewed membership

The parent and nine reviewed subassets were issued on June 2, 2018 by the same issuer. Their metadata remains available at bitcoinlt.org. The parent explicitly identifies the Bank of Lithuania digital collector coin hackathon and links to coin.orcaalliance.eu; the bank independently lists ORCA Alliance as a participating team. This is a hackathon prototype, not a claim of official central-bank issuance.

Sources:
- [Bank of Lithuania participant announcement](https://www.lb.lt/en/news/eight-teams-to-participate-at-the-bank-of-lithuania-hackathon) (used as the surviving collection website).
- [Original parent metadata](http://bitcoinlt.org/json/litocoin.json).
- Original project URL recorded in that metadata: https://coin.orcaalliance.eu (unavailable during review).

Use the on-chain longnames exactly. Subasset JSON fields omit the LITOCOIN prefix. Mažvydas metadata also says MARTYNAS_MAZVYDAS_5_EUR, but the actual asset is LITOCOIN.MARTYNAS_MAZVYDAS_10_EUR; do not rewrite it from that metadata. The biographies describe the portrait subjects, not their artists. No Artist traits are inferred.

This is a reviewed static membership snapshot. Additional prefix matches are not automatically admitted. All 10 entries have stored CDN artwork. These assets use this collection as their primary home and are removed from the Counterparty catch-all.
