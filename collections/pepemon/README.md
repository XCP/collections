## Membership evidence

The original Counterparty Pepemon catalogue is preserved in the March 15, 2022 archive of https://pepemon.net/static/js/main.606ec2f1.js at https://web.archive.org/web/20220315081421id_/https://pepemon.net/static/js/main.606ec2f1.js . Its embedded cardList contains all 33 exact names, numbered 1–33, original artwork URLs and artist credits. The JSON data was extracted without executing the archived code. This is the pepemon.net project, distinct from similarly named Ethereum projects.

All names were resolved to exact xcpio-core identities and all 33 original artwork previews inspected. The matching cards remain available at their catalogue Imgur URLs. The nine existing Counterparty memberships are removed; all other cards receive their documented collection home. Collection website records the original project domain, currently unavailable; the archive is the membership evidence.

Collaboration credits are separate Artist traits: burstMembrane and CelestialGreen; Majoni and Notgod. Existing artist credits are retained, with established profile names used for case/alias normalization. Zed and ejay resolve through the marketplace's existing merges to Zed Erwan and Rarejay.

BLAZIKENPEPE membership is certain, but its artist remains unassigned here: the catalogue says Joey while the card illustration line says REMSTER. Do not equate Joey with another artist based only on its issuer. Full animation review remains open; the catalogue establishes membership independently of imagery.
