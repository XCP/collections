## Membership evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-13. Crypsi names three related Pac-Man-inspired artworks:

- [Bitman - Banks](https://crypsi.com/pacman-banks/)
- [Bitman - Dark Markets](https://crypsi.com/dark-pacman/)
- [Bitman - Fiat Currencies](https://crypsi.com/pacman-currency/)

The artist lists all three as spray on canvas, 30 × 40 cm, original year 2017 and physical edition of six. This registry groups their 15 previously reviewed Counterparty tokens into one collection. The published subtitle is retained as the Theme trait: Banks, Dark Markets or Fiat Currencies. The old three collections unnecessarily separated these subjects.

All 15 exact token identities, primary memberships and Crypsi credits are preserved from the 2026-10-09 xcpio-core and CDN review (issuer 16qe8o8EjugUJCVTdD8mF3atPCfYZwSStB). PAC-prefixed and abbreviated names remain separate tokens. This is not the superhero Bitman in Superheroes of Crypto. Physical edition counts do not establish on-chain membership or redemption rights.
