## Membership evidence

The [official project website](https://nftcolony.space/) records the Counterparty release of Umeko Omiyage in 2023. Its space-travel storyline corresponds to the eight numbered Legendary Story of Umeko cards. The 24 selected cards come from issuer 165LuyEuVqCpcCaDeVrcxjscDurCAKdfUX; every CDN preview was inspected, and each explicitly depicts or names Umeko. Seven Omiyage cards retain their printed 01–07 numbering; the Legendary Story cards retain 00–07. Other related Umeko / Pepe cards are included without invented series or card numbers.

[Umeko's own Counterparty buying guide](https://note.com/umekospacecolony/n/n590642318e5f), published March 17, 2023, explicitly identifies PEPEUMEKO as a work she created and presents her Umeko Omiyage series. Those eight works receive the Umeko credit. The project works with multiple creators, so the remaining cards are not automatically credited from the issuer address. The guide and official homepage link the NFTsistersJAPAN social account.

## Boundaries

This entry covers the Counterparty cards, not every multichain Umeko release. Two numeric registrations from the address are excluded: one has no available preview, and the other is a separate pixel-head Stamp. Their membership and authorship need independent evidence. Current easyasset JSON endpoints are empty; neither artist attribution nor official-directory acceptance is inferred from those endpoints. All 24 included assets previously had only Counterparty fallback membership, which is removed.
