## Artist credit evidence

The October 2026 credit review uses the collection-operated [FAUX SOGS site](https://www.fauxsogs.wtf/) and its [full search catalog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/realcoinking/realcoinking.github.io/refs/heads/main/full-list.json), referenced by the site navigation. Asset names are matched to existing registry entries, using the catalog card link where its display name has a typo; membership and existing credits are unchanged. Card previews were reviewed as corroborating evidence.

Existing canonical artist names are retained, including previously merged aliases. D. Shorty maps to shorty, kapirasong.dayami to kapirasong-dayami, and handle prefixes are removed for the established AdeKrypto, M0d3d3b0 and MemeKingArt profiles. MEDICUS has separate AdeKrypto and AbysVora credits as the official catalog specifies. An issuing address is not treated as artist evidence. Unresolved catalog aliases and new artist identities remain for a separate review.
