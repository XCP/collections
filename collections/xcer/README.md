## Additional XPSA membership evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-10 against xcpio-core issuance history and CDN card images. XPSA explicitly describes itself as “XPSA Token by XCER” and its image presents the XPSA graded-card offering. MIKESCHMIDT’s original issuance describes “XCER.CO ASSET & PSA CARD”; its card identifies XCER and the bundled asset/token/PSA card, and the already-included XPSA.MIKESCHMIDT description explicitly includes the MIKESCHMIDT asset. These two assets therefore join the existing XCER collection and leave Counterparty, retaining their existing XCER credits.

Image references: https://cdn.xcp.io/img/card/XPSA and https://cdn.xcp.io/img/card/MIKESCHMIDT. This does not extend membership to unrelated assets merely because they share an issuer.
