This folder is the public record for this collection.

RARE is the family of onchain artworks by dxxmsdxy published at
[rare.directory](https://rare.directory/). Membership:

- `RARE`, the original 1/1 asset minted in April 2014, now carrying an onchain
  artwork.
- `RARE.PEPE`, Rare Nakamoto, the 10-year anniversary remaster of the Nakamoto
  Card (supply 300).
- `RARE.PEPE.1` through `RARE.PEPE.300`, the Rare Nakamoto 1/1 editions. The
  `Edition` trait is the number in the subasset name.

The divisible `FAKEBANG` token (Retroactive Pepenuity) is not a member.
Verify membership against the locked subassets of `RARE` on Counterparty and
the [editions gallery](https://rare.directory/editions/).
