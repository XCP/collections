## Membership

Includes HODLPET, its associated artworks, and the HODL Trumps card game.
The former `hodl-trumps` entry was consolidated here after curator review:
its 43 members were the remainder of the same family after 18 HODLTRUMPS
cards had already been included in HODLPET. That split reflected discovery
history rather than a separate project boundary.

Evidence: the [ARTISTE](https://cdn.xcp.io/img/full/HODLTRUMPS.ARTISTE),
[BASKETBALL](https://cdn.xcp.io/img/full/HODLTRUMPS.BASKETBALL), and
[RULEBOOK](https://cdn.xcp.io/img/full/HODLTRUMPS.RULEBOOK) cards carry HODLPET
branding, and RULEBOOK explicitly names HODL Trumps. The parent token's
[metadata](https://hodlpet.github.io/hodlpet/HODLPET.json) links the original
HODLPET project.

Keep reviewed artist credits on individual works. Shared branding or issuer
addresses do not establish a single artist for the entire collection.
This remains a reviewed membership list, not automatic admission of every
future HODLTRUMPS subasset.
