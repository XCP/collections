## Artist plates

Every CrystalsCraft card prints its artist on the plate beneath the title. Credits follow those plates exactly: Joe Chiappetta (22), Pharaoh (6), pbock (3), ICQPEPE (2) and Hirosu (SOGKO). GDCNOVADEMO's plate reads BitCrystals, the publisher, so it gets no artist credit. NICEWINGLIZ and SOGLOTUS keep their existing Shingo credits (plates read SWINGO).
