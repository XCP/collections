Eight tokenized renderings and documents from Adam Kadmon’s named series: Genesis, its video, I, III, IV, V, BR_WHITEPAPER and BRIV_POA. These become primary here and secondary in the VADAMKADMON overview.

[The creator’s 2022 auction](https://scarce.city/auctions/bitcoin-religion-v) explicitly identifies VADAMKADMON.BR_WHITEPAPER and VADAMKADMON.THE_BITCOIN_RELIGION_V. The [artist catalogue](https://scarce.city/creators/adam-kadmon) independently names the series. The other panels are matching renderings in the same exact named namespace; BRIV_POA visibly names Adam Kadmon and Bitcoin Religion IV. The whitepaper describes the series and bears the same signature.
