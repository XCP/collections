## Winking Thug

PEPETHUGLIFE.WINKINGTHUG.OG is credited to the existing JuanGalt identity. Its exact [chain-linked JSON](https://xcp.coindaddy.io/A257172113726525928.json) explicitly names Juan Galt, and the artwork is signed Juan Galt. The parent and INCARNATE.I edition already credit him. This replaces the unsupported Pepenardo credit only on this work; no global Pepenardo rewrite is appropriate.

The parent and two illustrated children form the Pepe Thug Life family. The original Rare Pepe primary membership is preserved, the family is secondary there, and the children leave Counterparty. INCARNATE.I [metadata](https://xcp.coindaddy.io/A17793371190631651408.json) identifies Johnny Dollar as maker of the associated physical work, which does not by itself make him a creator of this digital card. The linked historical article currently returns 404 and is not used as evidence.
