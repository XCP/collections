# Complete artist date coverage, 2026-10-13

## Scope and result

Provide an evidenced first-work year or a documented first-use estimate for every one of the 1,176 artists in the complete public directory snapshot. The prior batch covered only 49. This pass supplies a dating anchor for all 1,176 without restoring the registration-date fallback.

Read all portfolios to cursor exhaustion, seed candidates from their five earliest registered works, reuse previous individual chronology reviews, and inspect all additional unchecked works registered before the best candidate year. The candidate/history cache contains 4,260 histories, including earlier collection audits. The additional older-work pass improved Majoni, Cornholio the Great and Kevin Ferreira. This is complete artist-level date coverage, not a claim that every work or every historical artist debut has been independently proven.

New date traits are applied to 1205 membership rows for 1149 distinct works across 218 collections. Existing Art year values, artist credits, memberships, primary flags and raw chain dates are retained. Kaleidoscope’s two adapter-only work dates are pinned alongside reviewed artist credits so a source refresh cannot erase them.

## Meaning and evidence standards

The sortable year is the earliest currently supported artwork activity in the tracked Counterparty portfolio. Some years are estimates; it is not necessarily the artist’s first artwork anywhere, first token reservation, first ownership of a token, or the founding date of a collection. A date on an old painting or a historical source image does not backdate its new Counterparty rendition.

A registration alone never supplies an anchor. Corroboration includes specific catalogue entries, original artwork-bearing descriptions, positive edition issuance, locking and contemporaneous project context. Invalid issuances are excluded. Later uploads can be repairs: original game-card descriptions and catalogue evidence take precedence over much later metadata migrations. Existing individually reviewed later-use overrides remain in place.

For the cases labelled first-documented-media-estimate, the date is a conservative bound supported by the named tracked artwork, an existing edition and its recorded artwork reference; it must not be presented as a verified creation date. Stronger older release evidence should move it earlier. This limited estimate is preferable to claiming OG status from a blank earlier reservation.
