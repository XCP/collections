# Artist OG chronology review, 2026-10-13

## Ranking policy

Artist OG means the earliest evidenced release of a work credited to that artist in the tracked Counterparty catalogue. It is not the age of their oldest token, the founding date of a collection they joined, or the beginning of their off-chain career. An old source image does not backdate a later derivative or edition. A collector, token issuer or later owner must not inherit an artist date merely through ownership.

Keep reasonable first-use heuristics: artwork-bearing descriptions or metadata, edition creation, supply locks, distribution and contemporaneous project activity may corroborate each other. A lone registration, transfer, JSON migration or current mutable URL cannot establish an OG year. Early Rare Pepes need catalogue/release evidence even when their descriptions omit images. Preserve existing supported later-use overrides.

The live ranking query already uses explicit Art year traits only, without registration or collection-founded fallback. This protects against spurious early dates but sparse traits also cause misleading omissions: the complete 1,176-artist snapshot has 38 dated artists and 1,138 undated artists. A minimum over a partially dated portfolio is only the earliest currently evidenced work, not a proven debut.

## First evidence-backed correction batch

Add 2016 Art year to 52 individually named original Rare Pepe artworks. Existing artist credits, other traits, membership and raw chain dates are unchanged. This restores early-work evidence for 14 artist identities, including three whose existing rank was misleadingly recent.

- [Series One auction catalogue](https://scarce.city/auctions/holy-grail-s1) explicitly dates the set to 2016 and names the 48 included artworks. The excluded ONLYONEPEPE and JONGPEPE are not assigned dates in this batch. The fractional LORDKEK holding still identifies the same artwork.

- [Mike original cards](https://scarce.city/auctions/RAREPEPE) independently supports the 2016 RAREPEPE and GOXPEPE artworks, distinguished from later physical editions.
- Each selected asset was checked against its complete valid xcp.io issuance history; all have contemporaneous 2016 activity. Chain history corroborates the catalogue dating rather than replacing it.

## Remaining scope at the first batch

Superseded by the [complete artist date coverage pass](artist-date-coverage-2026-10-13.md), which provides a documented date or estimate for every artist in the snapshot.

This is not a completed audit of artist chronology. The collection review did not establish the debut of every artist. After this batch, the expected directory coverage is 49 dated artists and 1,127 undated artists. Remaining work includes checking the earliest candidate works of the other artists, improving dates currently driven only by later works, and separating evidence of an original artwork from later editions or reuses. No dates are fabricated to fill this gap.

Future corrections should record the exact credited work, release year, source and reasoning here or in a linked evidence report before adding Art year. Do not restore an issuance/founded fallback to fill missing dates.
