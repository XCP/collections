# Art years from first use, 2026-10-12

An asset registered years before it carried art must not date its collection or artist to the registration year. Evidence used, in order: the issuance whose description first points at art (image URL, metadata JSON, stamp payload); the first send, dispenser or DEX match; the collection's own founded year.

Rules: a correction needs the art-bearing description two or more years after the date the asset is currently given (issue year, raised to the collection's founded year). Directory-hosted sets (Rare Pepe, Spells of Genesis, Fake Rares, Faux Sogs, Fake Commons, Dank Rares, Age of Chains) keep their issue year when issued during the project's life, because their art lived on directory sites. Any asset traded before its description showed art keeps its issue year: collectors already had the art. 44 candidates were kept for that reason.

## M.O.A.A

All 85 names were registered in 2015 (seven moved once that year, none since) and first carry art in XCPinata's 2022 descriptions, so `founded` is 2022.
