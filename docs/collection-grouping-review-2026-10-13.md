# Collection grouping review — 2026-10-13

## Scope

Screened the metadata inventory of all 1,115 registry entries and searched the stored membership evidence for grouping intent. Conducted focused source review for the 35 additional classifications below, using existing documented metadata/artwork reviews and rechecking representative creator websites. This is not a new asset-by-asset media audit or a claim that every external source was fetched again.

- 431 existing `variation_of` classifications retained.
- 5 existing explicit editorial labels retained.
- 35 additional labels: 26 Series, 8 Set, 1 Curation.
- 644 entries remain without a special badge. Absence of a label is not certification of an original official collection.

## Judgement rules

Use Series for an evidenced named sequence or ongoing artistic body; Set for works with a discernible joint purpose, finite composition, or collecting relationship; Curation for a retrospective grouping assembled for discovery. Numbering, printed titles, creator catalogue headings, narrative continuity and collector rewards can support intent. Shared issuers and subasset namespaces alone do not. A Set label does not promise completeness. A descriptive registry title does not by itself make an original related group a retrospective curation.

Reconstructing a real project checklist is different from assembling a new grouping. Existing `kind` and primary membership are deliberately independent of these labels. No membership, chronology, website or artist changes accompany this review.

## Important decisions to leave unbadged

- **Rare Pepe, Fake Rares, Spells of Genesis and other established projects:** their internal numbered series do not make the whole project a Series badge candidate.
- **Kaleidoscope:** an independently operated open art gallery/project with its own membership API, not a retrospective selection assembled by this registry.
- **Sharp Cards:** its checklist is reconstructed, but the project name exists in original artwork and linked site references. Reconstruction alone is not a Curation label.
- **Weird n’ Wild:** JP Janssen explicitly published the named token group; use of adapted source imagery does not make it a registry curation.
- **XCPinata Miscellaneous Works:** this is a category in the creator catalogue. Do not imply the registry invented it.
- **Pepebrick:** a work family spanning Rare Pepe, Fake Rares and companion tokens. Do not label it a subset of either directory or a one-parent namespace variation.
- **VADAMKADMON and YOUL:** namespace/portfolio overviews; neither a single series nor a retrospective selection by default. Their descriptions carry that distinction; no new Overview badge introduced here.
- **Vizique’s Parody & Fun, Classic Characters and Icons, Beginnings and Player Cards and painter-inspired groups:** creator portfolio categories do not necessarily assert a discrete series. Explicitly named series are labeled; thematic portfolio sections are left unbadged.
- **VADAMKADMON Game Cards:** coherent gameplay cards with a descriptive title, but no established original game identity. Retain the existing explanatory description rather than claim an official named series.
- **Blockchain OG, Lil Pepe, Maskamoto, RPEPE Series and The Colours of Pepe:** their current descriptions suggest grouping intent, but membership README evidence is thin. No label inferred from the title alone in this pass.
